How to print it

Printed cans give the best result, including the bare-metal wordmark, but can makers set high minimum orders per design.

Shrink sleeves or printed labels on blank cans suit a launch with small runs. Sleeves can carry a metallic ink in place of bare aluminium.

Turning this into print files

This artwork is a concept at screen resolution. For print, rebuild it as vector files on your can maker's or label printer's template, with their bleed and safe zones.

Label checklist

For US retail. The design covers the required elements; a few need your real business details, and a food-labelling specialist should review the final files.

  • On label
    Product name on the front“Sparkling Energy Drink” under the wordmark, with the flavor name.
  • On label
    Net contents in the bottom 30% of the front“12 FL OZ (355 mL)” in both US and metric units.
  • On label
    Nutrition Facts panelLayout is in place. Replace the figures with your lab-tested values.
  • On label
    Ingredient listCheck the order matches your final formula, heaviest first.
  • On label
    Caffeine amount and warning160mg per can, plus who should avoid it.
  • To do
    Full distributor addressThe label says “Portland, OR”. Add the street address and ZIP unless your business is listed in a public directory.
  • To do
    Real barcodeGet a GS1 company prefix and a UPC for each flavor; the label shows a placeholder.
  • To do
    Deposit and recycling marksStates with can deposits (for example California, New York and Michigan) need their refund marks.
