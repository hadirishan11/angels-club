FAQ

Good
questions.

How many can I drink a day?
The FDA cites 400mg of caffeine a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects for healthy adults. One can is 160mg, so two cans is 320mg. Count your coffee and tea too.
Who shouldn't drink it?
TORQ isn't for children, anyone pregnant or nursing, or people sensitive to caffeine. If you take medication or have a heart condition, check with your doctor first.
Why allulose and monk fruit?
Allulose is a rare sugar that tastes like sugar but isn't counted as added sugar and barely affects blood glucose. A touch of monk fruit rounds it out with no aftertaste.
How does the subscription work?
You save 15% on every order, delivered every 4 weeks by default. Change the interval, skip a delivery or cancel from your account. No minimum commitment.

Three origins. One can.

TORQ started in a Portland kitchen in 2021 with one question: why does every energy drink taste like melted candy? Five years and five flavors later, we still taste every batch.

Where it comes from

Sourced, not synthesized.

Guayusa

Grown in the shade of the rainforest canopy by a Kichwa farming cooperative, who are paid above fair-trade minimums for every kilo.

33.56° N, 133.53° E
KŌCHI, JAPAN

Yuzu

Kōchi prefecture grows roughly half of Japan's yuzu. Ours is pressed whole, peel included, which is where the floral bitterness comes from.

37.50° N, 15.09° E
CATANIA, SICILY

Blood orange

Tarocco oranges from the slopes of Mount Etna. Cold nights and warm days are what turn the flesh deep red.
