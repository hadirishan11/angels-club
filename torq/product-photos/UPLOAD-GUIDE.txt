TORQ PRODUCT PHOTOS
===================

27 images, 2048 x 2048 px JPG (Shopify's recommended square size).

HOW TO ADD THEM IN SHOPIFY
--------------------------
1. Products > TORQ Sparkling Energy > Media: upload all 25 flavor photos.
   Drag the Blood Orange hero photo to the first position; it becomes the main product image.
2. Variants: open each variant and choose its image. Use the flavor's 01-hero photo
   (for example, every Lime + Matcha variant uses torq-lime-01-hero.jpg).
   When a shopper picks a flavor, Shopify then shows that flavor's photo.
3. For each image, click it and paste the alt text below (helps accessibility and search).
4. The two lineup photos are for collections, the home page or ads:
   Products > Collections > (collection) > Image, or the theme editor.
