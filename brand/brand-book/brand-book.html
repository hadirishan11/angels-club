01 · Overview

About this document

Prestige Vault sells luxury shadow-box frames: a black velvet display holding a prop cash stack, Prestige Vault packs and sports-car style key fobs. This document sets out how our brand must appear on every product and package, and the exact specification for each item we order.

Mandatory requirements

  • No third-party brands or logos anywhere. Do not use Marlboro, Ferrari, Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, Lamborghini, BMW or any other company's name, logo, emblem or packaging design on any item, including key fobs, packs and stickers.
  • All cigarette-style packs must use the Prestige Vault pack design in section 06 only.
  • All key fobs must be unbranded: no car emblems, shields or badges.
  • Prop money must be printed on one side only and be smaller than 75% of a real banknote (US $100 note: 156 × 66 mm, so maximum 117 × 49 mm).
  • A physical or photographed sample must be approved by Prestige Vault before mass production.

If any requirement cannot be met, please tell us before production starts.

02 · Logo

The Keyhole Crest

Our logo is a gold keyhole inside a double diamond, with the name PRESTIGE VAULT. Vector files (SVG) and high-resolution PNGs are supplied with this document.

Primary logo. Gold on black. Use on gift boxes, cards, stickers.
Light version. Black on cream or white backgrounds.
Horizontal logo. For long, narrow areas (box sides, labels).
Crest only. For small items: frame stickers, pack fronts, foil stamps.

Usage rules

RuleRequirement
Clear spaceKeep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the keyhole on all sides.
Minimum sizeFull logo: 30 mm wide. Crest only: 10 mm wide (foil: 12 mm).
ColorsGold on black, or black on cream/white only. Gold foil is preferred over printed gold.
Do notStretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, change the font, or place on busy photos.

06 · Custom inserts

Packs, band, fobs and plaque

Prestige Vault pack

  • Replaces all branded cigarette packs. Same size as your current mini pack.
  • Matte black body, Vault Gold top band and thin gold edge line.
  • Front: crest and PRESTIGE / VAULT. Back: crest only. No health warnings, no barcodes, no other text.
  • Open pack: lid open, showing prop cigarettes or rolled prop notes. Closed pack: lid shut.

Money band

  • Vault Gold paper band, centered on the stack, text in Cinzel.
  • Standard text: $10,000. Legacy: customer's name, uppercase, max 18 characters.

Legacy plaque

  • Brushed gold metal, approx. 80 × 16 mm, adhesive back, centered below the key fobs.
  • Laser engraved in Cinzel, uppercase, max 18 characters.

08 · Orders & quality

Personalised orders, samples and QC

Personalised (Legacy) orders

  • We send the name for each order in writing. Copy it exactly: spelling, spacing and punctuation.
  • Engraving and band text are always UPPERCASE in Cinzel.
  • Send one photo of the finished frame before shipping each personalised order.

Sample approval

  • Before the first production run, send one sample of each composition (Starter, Boss, Legacy) or clear photos and a video.
  • Mass production starts only after written approval from Prestige Vault.
  • Any change to materials, colors or suppliers needs new approval.
